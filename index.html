How this is calculated

Map colors come from drive-time zones in 5-minute steps, calculated for a typical upcoming Tuesday so traffic patterns are realistic.

Evening zones are measured leaving work at your end time. Morning zones are an estimate: Mapbox only measures outward from a point, so they use traffic leaving work 30 minutes before your start time.

Hover any area for exact times: a real "arrive by" route for the morning and "leave at" route for the evening, plus the neighborhood name.

Changing the limit or color mode is instant. Changing a time only re-fetches that one leg, and every result is cached in your browser.
